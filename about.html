About the Creator

👋 Hey there!
I’m Aniket Bhatikar, someone who is deeply curious about algorithms, design, and the fascinating ways things work.

I come from Goa, India, a place known for its beaches ⛱️🌊, love for football ⚽, and some truly amazing food 😋. Growing up here taught me to enjoy exploring the world around me.

Exploration for me is not only about places 🌍. I love exploring ideas, meeting new people, and understanding the small details that make things tick 🔍. Science, technology ⚙️, creativity, and even the unexpected twists of life 🍃 all spark my curiosity.

At the heart of it, I simply enjoy figuring things out and learning something new along the way.

I’ve always been someone who enjoys thinking deeply 🤔. Not the kind of thinking that turns into endless overthinking… although that happens sometimes too 😅. It’s more a quiet curiosity about the world.

Questions tend to pop into my mind all the time. How can something small and simple grow into something extraordinary?

I don’t always find clear answers, and honestly that’s part of the fun. The real excitement lies in the process of wondering, exploring, and slowly piecing things together 🤩.

This website is my space to document my journey and the things I find interesting along the way. There’s no strict theme here, just experiences, adventures, reflections, and thoughts that cross my mind 💭. Some posts go deeper, some stay light and casual, but each one captures a small step in how life keeps unfolding 🌱.

I love watching football matches, biased more towards my country. Since the age of 7, I've been a 12th Gaur of FC Goa (my fav), a club of memories and emotions! Matchdays always bring a different kind of energy 🔥. Supporting our Gaurs as they play on the field for 90 minutes and beyond is absolute cinema! It's something you really have to experience to understand.

Another thing about me? I love cooking and eating food... 🍛🥘 A lot! Good food has a way of making everything better, whether it’s a plate of something comforting or a new dish I’m trying for the first time. If we ever meet, don’t be surprised if food becomes a major part of our conversation.😂

I also enjoy traveling🧳, not in a checklist way, but more in the sense of exploring new places🗺️, cultures, and people. Sometimes, the best moments aren’t the planned ones but the ones that just happen along the way😇. Life is full of those unexpected moments, and I love being in the middle of them!

This place is about my perspectives, experiences, and ideas that might resonate with you too. Stick around, read a bit, and who knows? You might find something that makes you think, smile or even see things a little differently.😉

Have a great day ahead!☺️

Dev bare karu!✨
